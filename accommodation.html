Stay at Bada Bhaktmaal Ashram

Bada Bhaktmaal Ashram provides peaceful and spiritual accommodation facilities for visiting devotees in Ayodhya. The stay experience is simple, disciplined, and spiritually enriching, designed to complement your pilgrimage to this sacred land.

Guidelines for Guests

🕐

Check-in & Check-out Times

Please coordinate with ashram administration for arrival and departure times. Early check-in or late check-out may be arranged based on availability.

📋

Required Documents

Valid ID proof is required for registration. Please carry original identification documents during your visit.

🙏

Ashram Discipline

We maintain a spiritually disciplined environment. Please respect ashram timings, participate in prayers if possible, and maintain the sanctity of the space.

🚫

Prohibited Items

Alcohol, tobacco, non-vegetarian food, and other prohibited items are not allowed within ashram premises.

About Maharaj ji

Mahanth Swami Shri Avdheshkumar Das Ji Maharaj is a revered spiritual leader of the Ramanandi tradition, guiding countless devotees on the path of Shri Ram Bhakti, devotion, and selfless service. His life embodies the principles of Sanatan Dharma and the sacred Bhaktamal parampara.

Under his divine guidance, Bada Bhaktmaal Ashram continues to serve as a beacon of spiritual light in Ayodhya, spreading the message of Lord Ram's devotion and organizing numerous religious and humanitarian activities throughout the year.

Teachings & Philosophy

Maharaj ji's teachings emphasize the importance of devotion (Bhakti), righteous living (Dharma), and selfless service (Seva) as the pillars of spiritual growth. His discourses blend traditional wisdom with practical guidance for modern devotees, making ancient teachings accessible and relevant.

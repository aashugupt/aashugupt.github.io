Frequently Asked Questions

Your Questions Answered

📍 Where is Bada Bhaktmaal Ashram located?

The ashram is located in Ayodhya, Uttar Pradesh, just 500 meters from Shri Ram Mandir. Address: Bada Bhakt Maal, near Jain Mandir, Ramghat, New Colony, Ayodhya, UP - 224123

🕉️ What are the Aarti timings?

Early Morning Aarti: 4:00 - 4:30 AM
Morning Aarti: 9:00 - 9:30 AM
Afternoon Aarti: 12:00 PM
Evening Aarti: 4:00 - 4:30 PM
Before Sleep Aarti: 8:00 - 8:15 PM

🏠 Is accommodation available at the ashram?

Yes, the ashram provides accommodation facilities for devotees. Please visit our Accommodation page for more details and booking information.

🍲 Is food provided at the ashram?

Yes, the ashram provides free Annadan Seva (free meals) for devotees and visitors. Simple vegetarian sattvic food is served with love and devotion.

💰 Can I make donations to the ashram?

Yes, donations are welcome and help support our various seva activities including Annadan, Gau Seva, Gurukul, and maintenance of the ashram. Please contact us for donation details.

🚗 How do I reach the ashram?

The ashram is well-connected by road, rail, and air. The nearest railway station is Ayodhya Junction, and the nearest airport is Ayodhya Airport. For detailed directions, visit our Transportation page.

📿 Are there regular Satsang and Katha programs?

Yes, the ashram regularly organizes Satsang, Shri Ram Katha, and spiritual discourses. Visit our Satsang page for current schedules and upcoming events.

👔 Is there a dress code?

We request devotees to dress modestly. Avoid shorts, sleeveless tops, and revealing clothing. Traditional Indian attire is appreciated but not mandatory.

📸 Can I take photographs inside the ashram?

Photography is allowed in designated areas. Please be respectful and ask permission before photographing deities, rituals, or people. Video recording may have restrictions.

💍 Can we organize Vivah Sanskara at the ashram?

Yes, the ashram facilitates Vivah Sanskara (traditional Hindu wedding ceremonies) according to Vedic traditions. Please visit our Vivah Sanskara page for more information.

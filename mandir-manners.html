Mandir Manners

Guidelines for a Respectful Temple Visit

Temple Etiquette & Guidelines

🙏 Before Entering

  • Remove your shoes before entering the temple premises
  • Dress modestly - avoid shorts, sleeveless tops, and revealing clothing
  • Switch off or silence your mobile phone
  • Wash your hands and feet if facilities are available

🕉️ Inside the Temple

  • Maintain silence and speak softly if necessary
  • Do not touch the deities or sacred objects without permission
  • Photography may be restricted - please ask before taking photos
  • Do not sit with your feet pointing towards the deity
  • Participate respectfully in prayers and rituals
  • Do not eat, drink, or chew inside the temple

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 For Families

  • Keep children close and ensure they remain respectful
  • Avoid bringing food or toys that may cause distraction
  • Use the designated areas for children if available

💰 Donations & Offerings

  • Donations are welcome but never mandatory
  • Use designated donation boxes
  • Prasad (blessed food) should be accepted with respect
  • Do not leave offerings in unauthorized places

❤️ General Courtesy

  • Respect other devotees' space and devotion
  • Avoid pushing or rushing during crowded times
  • Follow instructions from temple staff and volunteers
  • Keep the premises clean - use trash bins
  • Be patient during queues for darshan

Remember: The temple is a sacred space for spiritual connection. Your respectful behavior helps maintain the sanctity and peace of this holy place for all devotees.

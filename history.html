Our History

A journey of devotion, service, and spiritual tradition spanning generations

Journey Through Time

Bada Bhaktmaal Ashram's history is a testament to the enduring power of faith, devotion, and selfless service. From its roots in the Ramanandi tradition to its present-day role as a spiritual sanctuary, the ashram has continuously served devotees with unwavering dedication.

⚛️

Traditional Roots – Ramanandi Lineage

The spiritual foundation of Bada Bhaktmaal Ashram is rooted in the sacred Ramanandi Sampradaya, inspired by the Bhaktamal tradition of Shri Ram devotion and Vaishnav philosophy. This ancient lineage has been the guiding light of the ashram, shaping its values and practices.

🕉️

19th Century – Establishment in Ayodhya

Bada Bhaktmaal Ashram was established in the holy city of Ayodhya — the birthplace of Lord Shri Ram — as a center of bhakti, spiritual learning, and saintly tradition. The ashram was founded with the vision of creating a sanctuary for devotees seeking spiritual enlightenment and divine grace.

👁️

1950s–1980s – Expansion of Katha & Religious Activities

Over the years, the Ashram became a center for organizing Shri Ram Katha, Shrimad Bhagwat Katha, Yagya ceremonies, and major Vaishnav religious gatherings, attracting devotees from across India. This period marked significant growth in the ashram's spiritual activities and community outreach.

❤️

1990s – Growth of Annadan & Seva Traditions

Large-scale Annadan Seva, Gau Seva, and festival celebrations were expanded. The Ashram actively served saints and devotees during major religious occasions. "Annadan is Mahadan" became the guiding principle, with free meal distributions becoming a cornerstone of the ashram's service.

⬆️

2000s – Participation in Kumbh & National Outreach

Service camps during Kumbh Mela, Magh Mela, and other large gatherings were organized, extending the Ashram's presence beyond Ayodhya and reaching devotees nationwide. The ashram began providing blanket donations and humanitarian aid during major pilgrimages.

🌟

Present Day – A Living Spiritual Institution

Today, Bada Bhaktmaal Ashram continues its sacred mission under the divine guidance of Mahanth Swami Shri Avdheshkumar Das Ji Maharaj. Located just 500 meters from the newly consecrated Shri Ram Mandir, the ashram serves as a beacon of spiritual learning, devotion, and humanitarian service for devotees from India and around the world.

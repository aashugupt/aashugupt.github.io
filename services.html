Our Services

Religious & Spiritual Activities

The organization actively conducts and organizes various spiritual and religious programs to inspire devotion, moral values, and spiritual awareness among devotees.

📿

Shri Ram Katha

Regular spiritual discourses on the life and teachings of Lord Ram, inspiring devotion and righteous living.

📖

Shrimad Bhagwat Katha

Sacred recitations and discourses from the Bhagavata Purana, guiding devotees on the path of bhakti.

🔥

Yagya & Hawan Ceremonies

Traditional Vedic fire rituals performed for spiritual purification and divine blessings.

🙏

Daily Aarti and Satsang

Morning and evening prayer ceremonies with spiritual gatherings for devotees and seekers.

🎪

Spiritual Camps

Special camps during Kumbh & Magh Mela providing spiritual guidance and services to pilgrims.

🎉

Festival Celebrations

Vibrant celebrations of Hindu festivals based on the traditional calendar with devotional programs.

Annadan & Humanitarian Services

Bada Bhaktmaal Ashram believes that "Annadan is Mahadan" (food donation is the greatest charity)

🍲

Free Meal Distribution

Regular free meals for saints, sadhus, and devotees visiting the ashram

📿

Annadan During Katha

Food service during spiritual discourse programs and religious gatherings

🎪

Kumbh Mela Service

Large-scale food distribution during Kumbh Mela and major festivals

🎁

56 Bhog Offerings

Special prasad offerings on auspicious occasions and festivals

🧥

Blanket Donation

Winter blanket distribution drives during Kumbh Mela in Prayagraj

🤝

Family Support

Assistance for needy families during religious gatherings and events

Gaushala & Dharma Seva

Cow protection and Gaushala Seva form an essential part of the Ashram's mission. The organization promotes traditional values of cow care and preservation as described in Sanatan scriptures.

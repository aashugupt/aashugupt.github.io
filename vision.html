Our Spiritual Foundation

Bada Bhaktmaal Ashram stands not only as a place of worship, but as a living spiritual institution dedicated to devotion, tradition, and humanitarian service in the sacred land of Ayodhya. Through Shri Ram devotion, Vaishnav saint tradition, Bhajan, Kirtan & Satsang culture, and scriptural study, we strive to create a spiritually awakened society where Dharma guides life and devotion becomes a daily practice.

Seva (selfless service) is the highest expression of our devotion. We believe that serving humanity is serving the divine, and through Annadan, Gaushala Seva, and spiritual guidance, we work to uplift both the spiritual and material well-being of all who come to us.

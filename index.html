About Bada Bhaktmaal Ashram

Bada Bhaktmaal Ashram is a revered Vaishnav spiritual center in the sacred city of Ayodhya, dedicated to Shri Ram Bhakti, Seva, and the preservation of Sanatan Dharma. Rooted in the Bhaktamal tradition and Ramanandi lineage, the Ashram serves as a place of devotion, discipline, and divine learning.

🕉️

Traditional Roots

🏛️

Sacred Location

Located just 500 meters from Shri Ram Mandir in the holy city of Ayodhya

Meet Maharaj ji

Mahanth Swami Shri Avdheshkumar Das Ji Maharaj

A revered spiritual leader of the Ramanandi tradition, guiding devotees through Shri Ram Katha, Bhakti, and selfless Seva. Under his divine guidance, Bada Bhaktmaal Ashram continues the legacy of Bhaktamal parampara through Katha, Satsang, and Seva.

Our History

⚛️

Traditional Roots – Ramanandi Lineage

The spiritual foundation rooted in the sacred Ramanandi Sampradaya, inspired by the Bhaktamal tradition

🕉️

19th Century – Establishment in Ayodhya

Established in the holy city of Ayodhya as a center of bhakti, spiritual learning, and saintly tradition

👁️

1950s–1980s – Expansion of Katha & Religious Activities

Became a center for organizing Shri Ram Katha, Yagya ceremonies, and major Vaishnav religious gatherings

❤️

1990s – Growth of Annadan & Seva Traditions

Large-scale Annadan Seva, Gau Seva, and festival celebrations were expanded

⬆️

2000s – Participation in Kumbh & National Outreach

Service camps during Kumbh Mela, extending the Ashram's presence nationwide
